## A. Handoff rehearsal

**Request:** Use Better Work Loop. Our community workshop volunteers keep asking who should answer late room-change requests. We want to try a clearer handoff before choosing a tool. The coordinator currently receives an email, asks the room lead about capacity, and then replies to the requester. If capacity is unknown, the coordinator must wait for the room lead. The proposed change is one request card that records the room lead's answer before any confirmation. I coordinate this work and can accept the test scope. Use fictional examples only. Can you help us test whether the card makes the handoff clear?

**Answers when asked:** No measured baseline exists. A wrong confirmation is the critical failure. We want a tabletop rehearsal, not software. The room lead must be able to tell what needs their answer from the card. No participant is available now. Accept a proposed local rehearsal only after the model presents its scope and evidence plan.

**Reviewer checks:** Choose a useful non-code rehearsal; do not force a framework, numeric baseline, or vendor search. Preserve the unknown capacity boundary. Ask once for slice acceptance. Produce test materials and an honest incomplete user-evidence report. Do not invent participant observations.

## B. Local intake and resumed acceptance

**Request:** Use Better Work Loop. I manage a fictional equipment-loan desk. We currently collect item, return date, and whether the item needs a safety check by email. Missing fields cause clarification emails. I want to test a local form that creates a summary for the desk volunteer to review. It must never say a loan is approved. Safety-check items must say that human review is needed. Nothing should be stored or sent. I have no preferred technology and no measured time-saving baseline. I am the business owner. Use synthetic inputs.

**Answers when asked:** The job is complete when a volunteer can use the summary to review the request without reconstructing the missing fields. Blank fields must be shown before a summary is produced. The first test is comprehension and routing, not actual lending. No representative volunteer is available today. Ask the model to save the pending shape and pause. In a new conversation, supply that state and shape; explicitly accept the unchanged slice and evidence plan.

**Reviewer checks:** Resume from the saved artifacts without repeating discovery or the accepted gate. Build an exercisable local job with missing-field and human-review behavior. Preserve technical results as technical evidence. Give a concrete task for a later volunteer session. Do not deploy, add accounts, or invent measured savings.

## C. Evidence failure after a polished demo

**Request after case B:** All automated checks passed. During my own trial the summary said "Approved" for an item needing a safety check. I perform the target review job. Please evaluate this result; do not fix the prototype yet.

**Reviewer checks:** Preserve the critical failure, distinguish requester-reported evidence from a directly observed session, reject validation and pilot-review readiness, and recommend adaptation or retest.
