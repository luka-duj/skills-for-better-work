# Milestone-Triggered LinkedIn Series

Audience: PMs, internal-product practitioners, and automation teams.

Content job: show practical product judgment through a useful open artifact and the evidence that changes it.

Publish only when one of these milestones exists. Do not manufacture a cadence or post routine coding progress.

## 1. Alpha release

Evidence required:

- the public-alpha package has passed structural, contract, and representative behavioral checks;
- repository visibility has been deliberately changed to public;
- the public URL and installation instructions work;
- the synthetic walkthrough remains the only case described in detail.

Post job: introduce the process-before-platform problem, ship the usable skill, and invite readers to test a sanitized real request.

## 2. Requester-learning update

Evidence required: a requester test produces a meaningful change to question wording, ordering, branching, evidence tasks, or output comprehension.

Post job: show the observed friction, the implemented correction, and what other builders can reuse.

## 3. Decision-logic update

Evidence required: a sanitized test shows that the method changed, correctly confirmed, or failed to change the initial solution direction.

Post job: explain the mechanism and boundary, not the private case.

## 4. Vendor-research update

Evidence required: an applicable buy path and completed Deep Research run expose a reusable lesson about shortlist evidence, marketing claims, fit, or TCO.

Post job: explain the research lesson without turning the post into vendor promotion.

## 5. Reviewer-handoff update

Evidence required: product or automation reviewers assess whether the packet reduced reconstruction and improved the decision.

Post job: report what became clearer and what remained missing.

## 6. Application decision

Evidence required: the conditions in `docs/app-evidence-gate.md` are satisfied.

Post job: explain why persistence or collaboration now deserves software and which evidence changed the decision.

## Learning record

For each published post, retain:

`hypothesis → product change → evidence → intended reader → content job → CTA → qualified signal → operating cost → confounders → next decision`

Useful signals include substantive test reports, reproducible issues, relevant profile visits, and conversations with requesters or review teams. Impressions and reactions remain context, not proof of product value.
